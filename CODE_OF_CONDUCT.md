# Code of Conduct

VClasp welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Treat participants with respect and keep technical discussion
focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and constructive suggestions.
- Give credit for others' work and respect their time and privacy.
- Accept corrections and help make discussions accessible to new contributors.
- Respect reasonable requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Criticism of code or research should address the work without
personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly
represents the project in a public setting.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainer privately at [antio2@qq.com](mailto:antio2@qq.com).
Include links or relevant context and describe any immediate concern.
Do not publish another person's private information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed with discretion. Information will be shared only
as needed to assess and address the report. Retaliation against someone
raising a concern in good faith is itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask participants to
change their behavior, restrict participation, or ban participants. Actions
should reflect the seriousness and recurrence of the behavior. The affected
person may use the same private contact to provide additional context or
request reconsideration.
